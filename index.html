01 / About

Who's building this

I'm a software developer who likes taking systems from “this could work” to “this runs in production.” My focus sits at the intersection of computer vision, real‑time web apps, and pragmatic engineering.

Most recently I built SafeDrive‑AI, a real‑time driver drowsiness detection system that watches eye closure, head pose and distraction through a webcam feed, streams results over WebSockets, and gracefully degrades from a MediaPipe pipeline to a lightweight Haar‑cascade fallback on constrained infrastructure. I care about the unglamorous parts too — deployment limits, silent failures, fallback logic — because that's where real products actually break.
